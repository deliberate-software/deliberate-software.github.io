Privacy, plainly.

Parker, the app

Parker isn’t out yet. This is how Parker will work when it ships.

Your notes stay with you

Every note is a Markdown file in a folder you choose, on your Mac or in your iCloud Drive. We never see them.

Nothing you write is sent to us

Parker doesn’t connect to the internet on its own; macOS doesn’t even allow it to. The only exceptions are Apple’s: buying Premium through the App Store, and opening links you click.

AI, on your Mac

Parker’s AI features use Apple Intelligence, right on your Mac. Your notes are never sent elsewhere to become training data for someone else’s model.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

You don’t sign up for anything, and Parker doesn’t track how you use it. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Buying Premium

Purchases go through Apple. Apple tells Parker that you have Premium, not who you are.

Protecting your notes further

To protect your notes if your Mac is lost or stolen, turn on FileVault. For notes in iCloud Drive, Advanced Data Protection adds end-to-end encryption.

This website and the waitlist

What we collect

Your email address, if you join the waitlist. That’s it. This site has no ads, no tracking, and no cookies of its own.

Who handles it

The waitlist runs on Kit, which stores your email address and sends our emails. Our web host, like any, sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page. Everything else is served from this site, fonts included.

What we do with it

We send you news about Parker: early access, the launch, and the occasional progress note. Nothing else. We never sell, rent, or share your email address.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted entirely, reply to any of our emails and ask. We’ll do it.

Changes

If any of this changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
