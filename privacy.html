Privacy, plainly.

Puli, the app

Puli isn’t out yet. This is how Puli will work when it ships.

Your notes stay with you

Your notes are kept on your Mac, and with Premium, synced through your iCloud account. We never see them.

Nothing you write is sent to us

Puli doesn’t connect to the internet on its own; macOS doesn’t even allow it to. The only exceptions are Apple’s: buying Premium through the App Store, and opening links you click.

AI, on your Mac

Puli doesn’t use AI today. When it does, it will use Apple Intelligence, on your Mac. Your notes will never be sent elsewhere to become training data for someone else’s model.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

You don’t sign up for anything, and Puli doesn’t track how you use it. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Buying Premium

Purchases go through Apple. Apple tells Puli that you have Premium, not who you are.

This website and the waitlist

What we collect

Your email address, if you join the waitlist. That’s it. This site has no ads, no tracking, and no cookies of its own.

Who handles it

The waitlist runs on Kit, which stores your email address and sends our emails. Our web host, like any, sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page. Everything else is served from this site, fonts included.

What we do with it

We send you news about Puli: early access, the launch, and the occasional progress note. Nothing else. We never sell, rent, or share your email address.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted entirely, reply to any of our emails and ask. We’ll do it.

Changes

If any of this changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
