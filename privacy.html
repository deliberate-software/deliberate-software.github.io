Deliberate Software · Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly.

This site collects one thing: your email address, if you join the waitlist.

What data do we collect?

Your email address, if you join the waitlist or write to us.

How do we use it?

We send you news about Puli: early access, the launch, and the occasional progress note. If you write to us, we write back. Nothing else.

Who do we share data with?

No one. We never sell, rent, or share your email address or anything else about you. Our waitlist runs on Kit, which stores your address and sends our emails. Our web host, like any, sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page. But we don’t collect, store, or share that information.

How long do we keep it?

Until you ask us to delete it.

What are your rights?

Ask what data we have, we’ll tell you. Ask us to delete it, we will.
