Privacy, plainly.

What we collect

Your email address, if you join the waitlist. That's it. This site has no ads, no tracking and no cookies of its own.

Who handles it

The waitlist runs on Kit, which stores your email address for us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page. Everything else is served from this site, fonts included.

What we do with it

We send you news about Graphite: early access, the launch, and the occasional progress note. Nothing else. We never sell, rent or share your email address.

Leaving

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted entirely, reply to any of our emails and ask. We'll do it.

Your notes

Graphite keeps your notes as plain Markdown files, stored where you choose. Before launch we'll publish Graphite's own privacy details here, including exactly what any AI feature sends, and where.

Changes

If any of this changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
