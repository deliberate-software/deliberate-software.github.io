We’re a small, independent software company. We make thoughtful apps for the Mac: private, calm, and made for everyone.

Our philosophy.

Your privacy is not for sale.

Plenty of software is free because you’re the product. Ours isn’t. Our apps keep your work on your Mac, encrypted, with no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. We can’t sell what we never collect, and we never will.

We won’t compete for your attention.

Too many apps are designed to keep you hooked. We design ours to protect your train of thought. They do their work quietly, with no badges, streaks, or ads. Your best thinking happens without interruption.

Your purchases are yours to keep.

Once you buy something from us, it’s yours to keep.

Everyone is welcome.
