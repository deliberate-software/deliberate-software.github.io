We’re a small, independent software company. We make thoughtful apps for the Mac, on purpose.

Our first app: Puli.

Puli is a notes app for the Mac.

It herds your thoughts into all the right places, so relevant context arrives just in time, right where it’s needed.

Meet Puli at getpuli.com →

Made on purpose.

Your privacy is not for sale.

Plenty of software is free because you’re the product. That’s not the case with us. Our apps keep your work on your Mac, encrypted, with no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. We can’t sell what we never collect.

We won’t compete for your attention.

Too many apps are designed to keep you hooked. We design ours to protect your train of thought. They do their work quietly, with no badges, streaks, or ads. Your best thinking happens without interruption.

Ownership should mean something.

Most subscription-based software takes everything away when you stop paying. Ours are different. Whatever you received while subscribed is yours to keep. We’d rather earn your renewal than hold features hostage.

We build for everyone.

However you use your Mac, you should find the whole app waiting for you, not a stripped-down version or an apology. When we build for everyone, everyone gets something better.

The best AI goes unnoticed.

AI should make life easier by augmenting what people already do well, not by replacing them. It should assist gently, in the background. When it’s working, you won’t notice it at all. You’ll just notice you got more done.
