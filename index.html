What Parker believes.

Your notes are yours

Every note is a Markdown file in a folder you choose, on your Mac or in your iCloud Drive. Open them in any editor, today or in twenty years.

Privacy is a given

Not a setting, and not a feature. Nothing you write is sent to us, sold, or used to train anything. When Parker answers a question, it answers on your Mac.

Made for everyone

Everything works from the keyboard and with VoiceOver, and the text grows to the size you need, from the editor to the context pane.

Calm, on purpose

No streaks, no badges, no nagging. Parker will never pull you out of your train of thought.

Subscriptions that keep their word

Most subscriptions take everything back the day you stop paying. Parker’s works like a magazine: what you’ve received is yours to keep.

Questions? Answered.

Is Parker really free?

Yes. Really. Everything you need to take notes and stay organized is free, forever. Premium is optional, and you can try it free for two weeks.

What do I keep if I stop paying for Premium?

Every Premium feature released while you subscribed. Like the issues of National Geographic on your shelf, Premium features are yours to keep after the subscription ends.

Where are my notes kept?

In a folder you choose, on your Mac or in iCloud Drive, as Markdown files. Parker reads them where they are, and changes made in other apps show up right away.

Does Parker use AI?

Parker’s AI features use Apple Intelligence, right on your Mac.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Parker starts on the Mac.

What Mac do I need?

Any Mac running macOS 15 or later. Ask Parker needs a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

Why I'm building Parker.

I’m a note taker. Like you, I’ve probably tried every notes app out there, and never stuck with any of them.

Notion is too cumbersome to set up. Obsidian gives me control, but no opinions, so I do all the work. Agenda came closest, with its calendar, but it still makes me file each note in one project. My notes don’t fit in one bucket.

So I built Parker for myself — for a job search, actually: who I was talking to, at which companies, about which roles. I enjoyed it so much, I wanted to use it everywhere.

Parker is fast and opinionated. It takes the work out of filing your notes, gives you context for every situation, and protects your privacy. And it’s free, with Premium features as spices, never the main ingredient.

I hope you enjoy using Parker as much as I do.

Alan Ridlehoover
Founder, Deliberate Software
