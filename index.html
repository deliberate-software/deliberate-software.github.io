Meet Puli.

A note-taker’s best friend.

You’re in meetings all day. You jot down who said what, about which project, and who promised what to whom. A week later, you’re in the same meetings, but you can’t remember who said what last week, and you can’t find your notes in the two minutes between meetings.

Puli is a different kind of note-taker. It’s friction-free. It herds each note into place, filing it under every person, organization, and project mentioned. It keeps watch over every promise. And it can even prep you for your next meeting when the time is right.

What Puli does for you.

Herds your notes

Mention a person, organization, or project, and Puli rounds up notes under each of them. One note, everywhere it belongs.

Comes when you call

Whistle with an @. Mention @maya, and everything you’ve written about her comes running, right next to your note.

Guards every promise

Jot down “Send @tim the deck” or “@maya will send the numbers,” and Puli keeps watch over both so you know what you owe and who owes you.

Questions? Answered.

What’s a Puli?

A Hungarian herding dog, famous for its corded coat. Quick, clever, and bred to keep the flock together. Say it POO-lee.

Is Puli really free?

Yes. Really. Everything you need to take notes and stay organized is free, forever. Lessons are optional, and you can try them free for 30 days. How Lessons Work.

What do I keep if I stop paying for lessons?

Puli never forgets a lesson. You keep every lesson Puli learned while you were a paying subscriber. Think of it like a magazine subscription. No one comes to take your back issues when you stop subscribing. Puli won’t either. How Lessons Work.

Where are my notes kept?

On your Mac, and if you choose, synced to your iCloud account, encrypted end to end. We never see them, and neither does anyone else.

Is there an iPhone app?

On its way. We’re a small, independent software company moving as fast as we can. Sign up for our mailing list, below, and we’ll let you know when it’s ready.

What Mac do I need?

Any Mac running macOS 26 or later.

Why I'm building Puli.

I’m a note taker. I’m guessing you are, too. And if you’re like me, you’ve probably tried every notes app there is, and never stuck with any of them.

Notion is too cumbersome to set up. Obsidian gives me control, but no opinions, so I have to do all the work. Bear came close, but there’s no sense of time. Agenda brings it all together, but it still makes me file each note in a single project. My notes don’t fit in one bucket.

So I built Puli for myself.

Puli is fast. Puli is opinionated. It herds your notes so you don’t have to. It fetches context for every situation. And it watches everyone’s promises. It’s private. It’s inclusive. And it’s free! (Lessons just teach Puli a few more tricks.)

I hope Puli becomes your best friend, too.

Alan Ridlehoover
Founder, Deliberate Software
