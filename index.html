Questions? Answered.

What’s a Puli?

A Hungarian herding dog, famous for its corded coat. Quick, clever, and bred to keep the flock together. Say it POO-lee.

Is Puli really free?

Yes. Really. Everything you need to take notes and stay organized is free, forever. Premium is optional, and you can try it free for two weeks.

What do I keep if I stop paying for Premium?

Every Premium feature released while you subscribed. Like the issues of National Geographic on your shelf, Premium features are yours to keep after the subscription ends.

Where are my notes kept?

On your Mac, and with Premium, synced through your iCloud account. We never see them.

Does Puli use AI?

Not yet. When we do teach Puli an AI trick, it will be a convenience, not begging for attention. Our plan is to use Apple Intelligence, right on your Mac. Nothing will leave the Apple ecosystem.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Puli starts on the Mac.

What Mac do I need?

Any Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 26 or later.

Why I'm building Puli.

I’m a note taker. I’m guessing you are, too. And if you’re like me, you’ve probably tried every notes app there is, and never stuck with any of them.

Notion is too cumbersome to set up. Obsidian gives me control, but no opinions, so I have to do all the work. Bear came close, but there’s no sense of time. Agenda brings it all together, but it still makes me file each note in a single project. My notes don’t fit in one bucket.

So I built Puli for myself — for a job search, actually. I needed to track who I was talking to, at which companies, about which roles. I enjoyed using it so much, I wanted to use it for meetings at work.

Puli is fast. Puli is opinionated. It herds your notes so you don’t have to, fetches context for every situation, and watches everyone’s promises. It’s private. It’s inclusive. And it’s free! (Premium just teaches Puli a few more tricks.)

I hope Puli becomes your best friend, too.

Alan Ridlehoover
Founder, Deliberate Software
