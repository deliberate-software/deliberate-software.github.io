# Deliberate Software: the brand

Notes to ourselves, not a page. This file is deliberate.software's brand guide; Puli's is `~/dev/puli-site/BRAND.md`. Where this file and the live site disagree, the site wins, and this file needs updating.

Copy is Alan's. Apply his line edits as he writes them, with the site's typography: curly quotes and apostrophes.

The company. Its site, deliberate.software, is one home page plus Privacy, Trademarks, Acknowledgements, and a 404.

## In one line

We're a small, independent software company. We make thoughtful apps for the Mac, on purpose.

## The name

- **Name:** Deliberate Software. The legal name is Deliberate Software LLC.
- **Tagline:** Made on purpose. "On purpose" is what "deliberate" means, so the lede ends on it too.
- **The lockup:** the name over the tagline, both in Instrument Sans: the name at weight 500, the tagline small, at weight 600, in spaced capitals, muted. At the head of the home page it's large, with a small raised ™ and no tagline, since the "Made on purpose." section follows. On other pages and in the footer it's small (18px), with the tagline.

## The look

- **Colors:** graphite `#27282B` on warm white `#FBF8F3`, reversed at night. Amber `#8A4B08` is the one accent (light amber `#FAC775` at night). Recessed bands `#F3EEE5` (`#313236` at night), muted text `#5C5D61` (`#BDB9B2`), lines `#DCD7CF` (`#4A4B50`).
- **Type:** headings and the lede in Newsreader, weight 400; text in Instrument Sans. Both are self-hosted.
- No illustrations or screenshots.

## The voice

The company page states beliefs, not features. Write the belief and what it means for a person. Leave out product names, devices, and services. "I don't want our philosophy to become a feature list." (Alan, Nov 21)

The home page, top to bottom:

- **The lede,** above.
- **Our first app: Puli.** "Puli is a notes app for the Mac." Then a line on what it does, and a link to getpuli.com.
- **Made on purpose.** Five beliefs:
  1. Your privacy is not for sale.
  2. We won't compete for your attention.
  3. Ownership should mean something.
  4. We build for everyone.
  5. The best AI goes unnoticed.
- **Say hello.** In the company's "we," like the lede: "Good software gets better when people speak up. … Are we on the right track? How can we improve? Let us know at hello@deliberate.software."

When Alan asks for options, give three in distinct tones, each checked in the page: the heading on one line, the paragraph three to five lines, no single word left alone on the last line.
