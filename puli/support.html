Puli · Support

How can we help?

Write to us at {{ site.email }}. We read every email, and we answer it ourselves.

What to include

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Puli’s version, from About Puli in the Puli menu.
  • Your Mac’s model and macOS version, from About This Mac in the Apple menu.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Leave out anything you’d rather we didn’t see.

Lessons

How Lessons Work explains each plan, and what you keep.

Lessons missing on a new Mac? Choose Teach Puli New Tricks… from the Help menu, then Restore Purchases.

Cancel a subscription: open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings, and manage your subscriptions there.

Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
