Puli · User’s Guide · Lesson

Tasks.

See every task from every note in one place, sort, filter, and group them, and see what’s due wherever you are.

A lesson

Tasks is a lesson. It’s free in Job Search journals. Without it, you can still write, start, and complete tasks in your notes; see Tasks and promises. See How Lessons Work.

The Tasks view

Choose View › Tasks, press ⇧⌘T, or click Tasks in the sidebar. You see the open tasks from every note, with their priorities and due dates. Click Task, Priority, or Due at the top to sort by it.

Click a checkbox to complete a task. Double-click a task to change its words, and ↩ to open its note.

Add a task

Type in “Add a task”, above the tasks, and press ↩. Due dates and priorities work as in a note: Send @Diane-Okafor the deck by Fri P1. The task goes in today’s note, under Tasks.

Change a priority or a due date

Click a task’s priority or due date, or its “—”, and choose another. Puli changes it in the note.

To put tasks in your own order, drag one by the grab bar at the end of its row, or press ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓.

Filter and group

Use the dropdowns beside the Tasks heading:

  • Show: Open, In progress, or Done tasks.
  • Due: Past due, Today, This week, and more, each with how many.
  • Group by: Priority; Owner, to see what each person promised you; or a kind of tag, such as People or Projects.

The sidebar also shows In progress, Past due, and Due today beneath Tasks. Click one to see those tasks.

Dates and priorities in your notes

With Tasks, a due date shows as a pill, such as “Fri, Nov 13”, and a priority as a small label. A task past due shows in red.

Open tasks on a page

A person’s, group’s, or project’s page in the context pane shows its open tasks, above its notes. Click ↗ beside “Open tasks” to see them in the Tasks view.

What’s due, wherever you are

  • Click the dog in the menu bar to see what’s due today.
  • Puli’s widget shows What’s next: the tasks due and past due.
  • Ask Siri: “What’s due today in Puli?”
