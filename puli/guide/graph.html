Puli · User’s Guide

The graph.

See how the people, groups, and projects in your notes connect, through the notes they share.

The Graph is a lesson. Until Puli learns it, there’s no Graph in the cabinet or the View menu, and ⇧⌘G does nothing. See How lessons work.

Open the Graph

Choose View › Graph, press ⇧⌘G, or click Graph in the cabinet. The Graph takes the editor’s place, with the context pane beside it.

Each person, group, and project tagged in a note is a dot, drawn as its icon in its kind’s color. Notes aren’t dots. Beneath “Graph”, a line says how many there are, as “48 people, orgs, and projects”.

If no note has a tag yet, the Graph says so. Tag people, groups, and projects in your notes, and they appear.

Read it

  • The more notes a dot is tagged in, the bigger it is.
  • The more notes two dots share, the closer they sit.
  • A faint line joins each dot to the ones it shares the most notes with.
  • Only the 10 most-tagged dots are named. Zoom in to see more names.

When you tag someone new, their dot joins the Graph without the others jumping.

Center a dot

Click a dot to center it. Its page shows in the context pane, and lines join it to its Top 10: the people, groups, and projects it shares the most notes with. The more notes they share, the closer they sit. Every other dot fades.

Point at one of its Top to see how many notes they share, as “42 notes with Robert”.

Click another dot to center it instead. To let go, click the empty space around the dots, or press ⎋. The context pane keeps the page it was showing.

The dot stays centered when you leave the Graph and come back.

Choose how many to show

The Top dropdown, at the top right, says “Top 10” at first. Choose 5, 10, 20, or All. When two share the same number of notes, Puli takes them A to Z. Your choice stays when you quit Puli and open it again.

Move around

  • Zoom with two fingers on the trackpad, or with + and − at the bottom right, or by pressing + and −.
  • Drag the empty space to move the whole Graph.
  • Drag a dot to move it. The dots tied to it follow.

The Graph opens zoomed to fill the window, and keeps filling it as you resize the window. Once you zoom, drag, or center a dot, it stays as you’ve put it until you let go of the center.

With the keyboard

  • Press an arrow key to ring a dot: the centered one, or else the most tagged.
  • Press ← → ↑ ↓ to move the ring to the next dot that way. With a dot centered, the ring moves only among it and its Top.
  • Press ↩ to center the ringed dot and show its page.

Find a dot

Type in Search, in the toolbar. The dots that match are named and colored, with their lines, and the rest fade to gray where they are. The most tagged match is ringed. Capitals and accents don’t matter, so “jose” finds José.

Press ↓ to ring a match, and ↩ to center it. Press ⎋ to clear Search. The centered dot stays centered.

Narrow the Graph

Click a name in the cabinet, and the Graph centers on it. With the Filters lesson, you can also show only some kinds, only those with open tasks, or only those active lately. See Filters.
