Puli · User’s Guide

Making Puli yours.

How to change Puli’s appearance and text size, clear everything away but the note, and get around without the mouse.

Light and dark

Choose Puli › Settings…, or press ⌘,, and look under General. Appearance shows three pictures of a Puli page: Light, Dark, and Match System. Click one.

Match System follows your Mac, and changes when your Mac does. Until you choose, Puli matches your Mac. Puli remembers your choice when you quit.

Each journal can also have its own colors and fonts. See Colors and fonts.

Text size

Choose View › Zoom In, or press ⌘+, to make the text bigger. Choose View › Zoom Out, or press ⌘−, to make it smaller. Choose View › Actual Size, or press ⌘0, to go back to 100%.

Each step is 15%, from 85% to 160%. Zoom changes the text of the note in the editor. The note’s column widens with it. The cabinet, the list of notes, the views, and the context pane stay their usual size. Puli remembers your text size when you quit.

Focus

To see only what you’re writing, choose View › Focus, press ⇧⌘F, or click the Focus button just right of Search. The cabinet, the list of notes, and the context pane close, and the toolbar slides up out of the way.

Move the pointer to the top of the window to bring the toolbar back. It slides away again when the pointer leaves.

While you’re focused:

  • You stay focused when you switch views, such as to Tasks.
  • Clicking a tag opens the context pane. Typing a tag doesn’t. Press ⌘\ to open it yourself.
  • Press ⌃⌘S to open the cabinet.

To leave, choose View › Leave Focus, press ⇧⌘F, or click the Focus button again. Everything comes back as it was. Puli doesn’t stay focused after you quit.

Accessibility

Puli follows the Mac’s own accessibility settings.

  • With Reduce Motion on in System Settings, the toolbar in Focus, and the panes that hide as the window narrows, appear and go at once, instead of sliding.
  • With VoiceOver, a task’s checkbox in the context pane and the Tasks view reads as Done, In progress, or Not done. In the Tasks view, VoiceOver also reads each task’s priority and due date, and which column the table is sorted by.
  • With Full Keyboard Access on, press ⇥ to reach the Tasks view’s dropdowns and Space to open one.

Move with the keyboard

Puli’s window has three panes beside the editor: the cabinet on the left, the list of notes, and the context pane on the right. You can hide each one from the View menu:

  • ⌃⌘S shows or hides the cabinet.
  • ⌃⌘L shows or hides the list of notes.
  • ⌘\ shows or hides the context pane.

The cabinet

Press ⇧⇥ from the list of notes, or click in the cabinet, to give it the keys.

  • ↑ ↓ move through the views, the Trash, and the names beneath each kind. Each one shows as you reach it. Your note stays as it is.
  • ← folds a section or a project, and → opens it again.
  • Type the first letters of a name to jump to it.
  • ⇥, or ↩, gives the keys to the view that’s showing.

The list of notes

  • ↑ ↓ move through the notes, each opening in the editor as you reach it.
  • ↩ or ⇥ puts the cursor in the editor, at the end of the note.
  • ⇧⇥ goes back to the cabinet.
  • ⌫ moves the note to the Trash. ⌘Z brings it back.

Menus

Press ⌃↩ to open the menu of whatever’s selected, as a Control-click would: a task in the Tasks view, a note in the list of notes, a person in the Tags view, a header in the cabinet, or a note in the Trash. Move through it with ↑ and ↓, and choose with ↩.

For every key in one place, see Keyboard shortcuts. You can also choose Help › Navigation for a short list in the context pane.
