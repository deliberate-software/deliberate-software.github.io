Puli · User’s Guide

Making Puli yours.

How to set Puli up the way you like it, clear everything away but the note, and get around without the mouse.

Settings

Choose Puli › Settings…, or press ⌘,.

  • General is for Puli as a whole: Light, Dark, or Match System; the day weeks start on; today’s note’s title; how long the Trash keeps things; and whether Puli shows in the menu bar.
  • Tags shows each kind’s mark, and how people’s names show: in full, or first names.
  • Privacy and iCloud: see Journals.

All but General are for the journal you have open. Lessons add more: Themes, Typography, and Calendar.

Text size

Choose View › Zoom In ⌘+ or Zoom Out ⌘− to change the size of the note you’re writing. View › Actual Size ⌘0 puts it back.

Focus

To see only what you’re writing, choose View › Focus, or press ⇧⌘F. Everything else closes, and the toolbar slides out of the way. Move the pointer to the top of the window to bring it back. Press ⇧⌘F again to leave.

Show and hide the panes

  • ⌃⌘S shows or hides the sidebar.
  • ⌃⌘L shows or hides the list of notes.
  • ⌘\ shows or hides the context pane.

Drag the line between two panes to make one wider.

Move with the keyboard

Press ⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→ to move between the sidebar, the list of notes, the editor, and the context pane. ↑ and ↓ move through what’s in a pane, and ↩ opens what’s selected. ⌃↩ opens its menu.

For every key, see Keyboard shortcuts.
