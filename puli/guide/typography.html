Puli · Handler’s Guide · Lesson

Typography.

Give each journal its own fonts, line spacing, and column width.

A lesson

Typography is a lesson. Without it, every journal is in Mixed. See How Lessons Work.

Choose the fonts

Open the journal, then Settings › Typography. Click a pairing of fonts for headings and text: Mixed, Book, Classic, Modern, System, Round, Airy, Elegant, Sans, or Writer. The journal changes at once.

Line spacing and the Focus column

Beneath the fonts, choose Line spacing, Tight, Normal, or Roomy, and how wide your note is in Focus: Narrow, Normal, or Wide.
