Puli · User’s Guide

Colors and fonts.

Give each journal its own colors and fonts, in light and dark.

Colors and Fonts are lessons. Until Puli learns them, Settings › Journal shows only Tags and Privacy, and every journal is in Cream and Mixed. See How lessons work.

Choose a journal’s theme

  1. Open the journal.
  2. Choose Puli › Settings…, or press ⌘,.
  3. Choose Journal, then Theme. The journal’s name shows above its settings.
  4. Click a color and a font.

The journal’s window changes at once. Your choice is marked in the settings.

Each journal keeps its own theme. Changing one journal leaves your others as they are. The theme goes with the journal, so it looks the same on your other Macs.

A new journal starts in Cream and Mixed.

Colors

Each color has a light and a dark version. Puli uses the one that matches Appearance, in Settings › General.

  • Cream: warm cream with graphite text, or graphite with warm cream text.
  • Blue: pale blue with navy text, or navy with pale blue text.
  • Green: pale green with forest text, or forest with pale green text.
  • Rose: dusty pink with plum-brown text, or plum-brown with pale pink text.
  • Lavender: soft violet with deep indigo text, or deep indigo with pale violet text.
  • Paper: white with near-black text, or near-black with white text.

Tags keep their kinds’ colors in every theme: people blue, groups green, and projects amber, each in a shade that suits the theme. Red still means only an error or a past-due date.

Fonts

  • Mixed: headings in Newsreader, a serif, and text in Instrument Sans.
  • Serif: headings and text in Newsreader.
  • Sans: headings and text in Instrument Sans.

The fonts apply everywhere the journal’s notes, tags, and tasks show: the editor, the list of notes, the context pane, the Tasks view, the Board, Tags, the Graph, and printed notes. Sizes don’t change.

What stays the same

The welcome window, About Puli, the Settings window, and a journal’s window while it’s locked are always in Cream and Mixed.
