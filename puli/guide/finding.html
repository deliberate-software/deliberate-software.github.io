Puli · Handler’s Guide

Finding your notes.

How to get back to what you wrote: by date, by who and what it mentions, or by the words in it.

The list of notes

The list of notes, left of the editor, holds your notes, newest first. Click one to open it. To see the list from another view, choose View › Notes, or press ⇧⌘N.

To keep a note at the top, choose Edit › Pin Note, or click the pin beside it in the list. To change the order, click the sort button in the toolbar.

The sidebar

The sidebar, at the left of the window, holds Puli’s views, then everyone and everything you’ve tagged, by kind. Click a view to see it. Untagged, Pinned, and Attached, under Notes, show just those notes.

Click a name, such as Diane Okafor, to see only her notes, and her page in the context pane. Click it again to see everything.

Search

Click Search in the toolbar, or press ⌥⌘F, and type. You see only the notes that have every word you typed. Names count, so “diane” finds every note that tags Diane Okafor.

Dates work too. Type “last week”, “October”, or “diane yesterday”. Press ⎋ to clear Search.

The Tags view

To see everyone and everything you’ve tagged in a table, choose View › Tags, or press ⇧⌘I. Choose a kind from the Show dropdown. Click a row to see its page, and a column’s heading to sort by it.

Spotlight

Turn on Show in Spotlight and Siri, in Settings › Privacy, and Spotlight finds your notes from anywhere on your Mac. It’s off at first, as Spotlight’s index isn’t encrypted.

The Trash

Notes and tags you delete go to the Trash. Choose View › Trash to see it. Select something and choose Restore to put it back, or Delete Now to delete it for good.
