Puli · User’s Guide

Importing notes.

Bring a folder of notes from another app, or one Puli exported, into a new journal.

Importing a folder

You can bring in a folder of Markdown notes from another app, or a folder Puli exported.

  1. In the welcome window, choose Import a Folder, then Continue. With a journal open, choose File › Import… instead.
  2. Choose the folder.
  3. Name the journal. The name starts as the folder’s.
  4. Leave Back up to iCloud on, or turn it off to keep the journal only on this Mac.
  5. Choose Import.

Puli counts the notes as it brings them in, then opens the new journal. The folder you imported doesn’t change.

What comes in

Each .md, .markdown, .txt, or .text file becomes a note, titled with its file’s name and dated when it last changed. Images and PDFs the notes show come along with them.

Notes from another app use the Imported notes template: @ tags people, and # makes a plain tag, which can nest, as in #work/budget.

A folder Puli exported comes back as it was, with its template, tags, stages, and Board. To export one, see Journals.
