Puli · Handler’s Guide

Importing notes.

How to bring the notes you already have into Puli.

Import your notes

Choose Import Notes in the welcome window, or File › Import… Choose where your notes are:

  • Apple Notes: every folder you choose, with its pictures. Your Mac asks you to let Puli read them first.
  • Bear: in Bear, choose File › Export Notes…, as Markdown with attachments, then choose that folder.
  • Agenda: in Agenda, export your projects as Agenda files, then choose that folder.
  • Obsidian: choose your vault.
  • A Folder of Markdown or plain text files.

Then choose to import into a new journal, or one you already have. Puli doesn’t change the notes you import from.

What comes with them

Your notes keep their dates, pictures, and checklists, which become tasks. Tags from other apps come along too. Apple Notes’ folders become tags, so each note is still easy to find.

Import again

Import the same notes into the same journal later, and Puli brings in only the ones it doesn’t have yet.
