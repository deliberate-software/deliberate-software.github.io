Puli · User’s Guide

Filters.

Show only the tasks that are past due, due today, or due this week, and narrow the cabinet, Progress, and the Graph to what’s open or recent.

Filters is a lesson. It comes with Groups. Until Puli learns it, the Tasks view shows every task, Progress every project, and the Graph every dot, and the filters below aren’t there. See How lessons work.

Filter tasks by due date

In the Tasks view, a dropdown beside the Show dropdown says what’s showing, as “All 27”. Choose from it:

  • All
  • Past due
  • Today
  • Tomorrow
  • This week
  • Next week
  • No due date

Each choice shows how many open tasks it holds. When Past due is chosen, it shows in the error color.

Sorting still works on what’s left. If nothing matches, the Tasks view says so, as “Nothing due today.” Puli remembers your choice when you quit and open it again.

If you add a task the filter hides, you see “Task added, hidden by this filter” at the bottom of the window.

Done tasks, by when they were done

Choose Done from the Show dropdown, and the same dropdown lists All, Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, and No date. No date holds tasks completed before Puli recorded when.

Past due and Today, in the cabinet

Beneath Tasks in the cabinet, Puli shows:

  • In progress, while a task is started.
  • Past due, while an open task is past due.
  • Today, while an open task is due today.

Each shows how many tasks it holds. Click one to open the Tasks view showing just those tasks.

The counts follow the tag chosen in the cabinet and what you type in Search, not the Tasks view’s due filter.

To hide them, click the chevron beside Tasks, or press ← on Tasks. Click it again, or press →, to show them.

Filter the cabinet

Hover over People, Orgs, or Projects in the cabinet and click its ⋮. Choose Filter › With open tasks. The section lists only those with open tasks, and its count says how many. Choose it again to list everyone.

The filter works with Sort by and Show. Filtered and set to Show 5, People lists the first 5 people with open tasks. Each section keeps its own choices.

Filter Progress

At the top right of Progress:

  • Nested under, once a project has projects inside it: All projects, or only the projects inside one.
  • Tasks: Any tasks, or With open tasks.
  • Active: Any time, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or Last 90 days.
  • With: Anyone, or one person. Type in its search field to find them.

A project is active when a note mentions it, or you change its page. With shows the projects mentioned on a line with that person.

The line beneath “Progress” says how many projects match, as “2 of 5 projects”. When none match, it says “No projects match.”, and the columns stay, so you can still drop cards in them.

Filter the Graph

At the top right of the Graph, before Top:

  • Show: check or uncheck People, Orgs, and Projects. One kind always stays checked.
  • Tasks: Any tasks, or With open tasks.
  • Active: Any time, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or Last 90 days.

A project counts as having open tasks if a project inside it does. The line beneath “Graph” says how many match, as “12 of 48 people, orgs, and projects”.

If you hide the dot you’ve centered, nothing is centered. See The graph.

Each journal keeps its own Progress and Graph filters, when you quit Puli and open it again.
