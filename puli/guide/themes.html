Puli · User’s Guide · Lesson

Themes.

Give each journal its own colors, in light and dark.

A lesson

Themes is a lesson. Without it, every journal is in Cream. See How Lessons Work.

Choose a theme

Open the journal, then Settings › Themes. Click a theme, and the journal changes at once. Choose from Cream, Paper, Mist, Slate, Sepia, Sage, Copper, Midnight, Plum, and Citrus.

Each theme has a light and a dark version, and Puli uses the one that matches Light, Dark, or Match System in Settings › General.

Each journal keeps its own theme, on all your devices. Tags keep their colors in every theme.
