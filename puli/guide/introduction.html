Puli · User’s Guide

Introducing Puli.

Most notes apps ask where a note belongs. Puli asks who and what a note describes. Tag them as you write, and your notes connect themselves.

One note, everywhere it belongs

A note from a meeting is rarely about one thing. It’s about the people in the room, the teams they’re on, and the projects they discussed. A folder can only keep it in one of those places. Choosing a single home for your note robs you of valuable context later.

In Puli, you don’t file notes. You tag them. Write @Diane-Okafor, !Development, and #Grant-Report in a note, and it shows up on Diane’s page, on the Development team’s page, and on the Grant Report project’s page. It’s one note, in three places, and you didn’t have to decide between them.

Tags connect your notes

Every tag is a thread. Write about Diane in January, in March, and in June, and her page gathers all three, newest first. Nothing you wrote about her is lost in an old notebook.

The threads connect the people, groups, and projects, too. Each tag’s page shows a list of the people, groups, and projects it’s mentioned with, and how often: who Diane works with, which projects keep coming up together, and which teams they involve. You never set any of that up. It comes from the way you write.

Because a tag stands for someone, not just a word, you can rename Diane, or give her an alias, and every note still finds her.

Context, when you need it

Before a meeting, you need to remember where you left off last week. Create a new note, type @Diane-Okafor, and her page opens in the context pane. Notes from your last meeting are there, as are the open tasks between you. Everything you need for your next meeting.

The pane follows your cursor. Move into #Grant-Report, and Grant Report’s page takes Diane’s place. You don’t search, and you don’t switch windows. The context comes to you, while you write.

Every promise, kept

Meetings end in promises: things you said you’d do, and things other people said they’d do for you. Write either as a task, right in your note:

  • - [ ] Send @Diane-Okafor the #Grant-Report update by Thu is yours.
  • - [ ] @Priya-Shah to send the #Grant-Report outcomes is Priya’s.

Puli keeps both. Priya’s page shows what she owes you, and Diane’s shows what you owe her. The next time either comes up, so do their promises.
