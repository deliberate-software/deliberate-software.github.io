Puli · Handler’s Guide

Tasks and promises.

How to write tasks in your notes, give them due dates and priorities, keep track of what other people promised you, and complete them.

Write a task

Type [] and a space at the start of a line, then the task: [] Call Tom. Puli shows a checkbox in its place. You can also use Markdown syntax directly: - [ ] Call Tom.

Press ↩ for the next task, and ↩ on an empty one to end the list.

Promises: yours and theirs

A task is yours unless it starts with a person. When it starts with someone, it’s a promise they made to you.

  • [] Send @Diane-Okafor the budget is yours.
  • [] @Diane-Okafor to send the numbers is Diane’s.

Give a task a due date

Write when it’s due, in words: [] Send the deck by Fri. When you save, Puli writes the date in its place, as in by 2026-11-13. Press ⌘Z right after saving to get your words back.

by or due works with a day, a date, or a word, such as by Friday, due Nov 20, or by next week. today, tomorrow, and EOW work on their own.

Give a task a priority

Write P0, P1, P2, or P3 anywhere in the task. P0 is the most urgent: [] P0 Fix the outage.

Complete a task

Click its checkbox. Puli notes the day you completed it. Click the checkbox again to reopen it.

To show you’ve started a task, ⌥-click its checkbox. The box shows half filled. ⌥-click again to stop.

See them all

With the Tasks lesson, Puli gathers the tasks from every note in one place, so you can sort them, filter them by when they’re due, and see what each person owes you.
