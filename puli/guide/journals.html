Puli · Handler’s Guide

Journals.

How to keep more than one journal, switch between them, back them up, keep them private, and tidy them away.

More than one journal

A journal keeps its own notes, tags, and settings. You might keep one for work and one for home. To start another, choose File › New Journal…, or press ⌥⌘N.

Switch journals

Choose File › Open Recent, or click the dog in the menu bar and choose a journal. To see them all, choose File › Open Journal…, or press ⌘O. Puli has one journal open at a time.

Back up to iCloud

Turn on Back up to iCloud, in Settings › iCloud. iCloud backs up the journal, encrypted end to end, and keeps it on all your devices. On another Mac, choose File › Open Journal… to find it under In iCloud.

Keep a journal private

Every journal is encrypted on your Mac, so only Puli can read it. For more, open Settings › Privacy:

  • Require Touch ID to open this journal asks for Touch ID, or your Mac’s password, before the journal opens.
  • Lock after locks it when you’ve been away for as long as you choose. It works once Touch ID is on.

To lock a journal that requires Touch ID now, choose File › Lock Journal, or press ⌥⌘L.

Manage your journals

Choose File › Manage Journals…, or press ⇧⌘J, to see every journal, on this Mac and in iCloud. Click a journal’s ⋮ to:

  • Rename it.
  • Archive it, for a journal you’re done with. It moves to Archived, out of your way.
  • Export it, as a folder of Markdown files any app can read.
  • Delete it. Choose Remove from This Mac to keep it in iCloud, or Delete from All Devices to delete it for good.

Close a journal

Choose File › Close Journal, or press ⌘W. Puli stays open. It opens the journal again the next time you open Puli.
