Puli

How lessons work.

Puli is free, forever. Lessons train Puli to perform new tricks.

Instincts

Puli is an instinctive herder. It comes with everything you need to keep your notes organized, from Markdown support, to tagging people, groups, and projects, to managing tasks, to fetching relevant context on command. Those features are free, forever.

Lessons

Lessons are how Puli learns new tricks. When you retain our trainers, Puli immediately learns every trick in our book. Plus, Puli never forgets a lesson you paid for, even if you stop paying for future lessons.

We offer two plans:

  • Our yearly plan includes every lesson in our book, plus 12 months of new lessons as they are released. This is a recurring plan that offers a 30-day free trial. You can cancel anytime.
  • Our forever home plan provides Puli with a lifetime of learning. Puli will learn every lesson we ever release, for a one-time, fixed price.

The lessons

There are seven lessons available to Puli as of our launch. They are:

  • Today: your day beside your notes, with calendar, tasks, and notes.
  • Tasks: every task from every note, sorted, filtered, and grouped.
  • Progress: track how projects move from To do to Done.
  • Graph: see how your notes connect through the tags they share.
  • Themes: ten color themes in both light and dark modes.
  • Typography: ten font pairings with custom line spacing.
  • Suggestions: Puli spots the tags and tasks you didn’t.

Job Search journals include Today, Tasks, Progress, and Graph for free.

Getting lessons

Choose Help › Take Puli for a Walk… to try a fully trained Puli on a sample journal. You won’t be able to save the journal. But you will be able to see all of Puli’s lessons.

Choose Help › Teach Puli New Tricks… to sign up for lessons. Or, if you’ve previously purchased lessons, click Restore Purchases.
