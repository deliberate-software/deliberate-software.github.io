Puli

How lessons work.

Puli is free. Forever. Lessons teach Puli new tricks. And, Puli never forgets.

A well-mannered pup

We tutored Puli extensively before your adoption. So, Puli knows all you’ll need to herd the notes you take about the people, groups, and projects in your life. Go ahead! Take a Puli for a walk around the block. We’re sure you’ll discover a well-mannered pup.

Lessons

For those interested in getting the most from their Puli, we also offer two professional training programs for your companion.

Yearly

In exchange for a small, recurring, annual fee, our staff will immediately teach your Puli every lesson in our catalog, plus every new lesson as it’s released, for 12 months. Cancel anytime. Puli will remember every trick it learned. Your first 30 days are free.

Lifetime

Or, for a fixed, one-time fee, you can provide your Puli a lifetime of learning. That’s every trick we teach. For the life of your pup.

In all seriousness (and plain English)

We hope you get real value from our free product. In fact, over time, we will continue to improve the base software. To fund that ongoing work, we offer premium features to enhance your experience with Puli. We call these features lessons.

When you subscribe to our Yearly plan, you will pay us a small, annually recurring fee for every premium feature we’ve released, plus all the new features we release in the following 12 months. You can cancel at any time. Puli will remember all the lessons it learned while you were paying us, even after you stop.

Think of it like a magazine subscription that gives you all the back issues on day one. You don’t know what the next issue will deliver. But you’re getting value immediately. And, no one will come confiscate the issues you’ve paid for and received when you cancel your subscription.

We also offer a Lifetime plan. Like the name implies, you’ll get every premium feature we ever release for a fixed, one-time fee.

The lessons

  • Today: your day beside your notes, with your meetings, what’s due, and a meeting’s page.
  • Tasks: every task from every note, in one place, with filtering and grouping.
  • Progress: track how projects move from To do to Done.
  • Graph: see how your notes connect through the tags they share.
  • Suggestions: Puli spots the tags and tasks you didn’t.
  • Themes: nine more color themes, light and dark.
  • Typography: nine more pairings of fonts, with line spacing and the Focus column.

Today, Tasks, Progress, and Graph are free in Job Search journals.

Getting lessons

Choose Help › Teach Puli New Tricks. You see the plans and every lesson. To see a lesson at work first, click its Try It in a Sample. If you’ve bought lessons before, on this Mac or another, click Restore Purchases.

Puli reminds you two days before your 30 free days end.

When lessons end

If your 30 free days end, or you stop paying before a lesson came out, that lesson’s features go away. Your notes don’t change. Sign up again, and everything comes back as you left it.
