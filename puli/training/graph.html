Puli · Trainer’s Guide

The Graph.

See how the people, groups, and projects in your notes connect, through the notes they share.

A lesson

Graph is a lesson. It’s free in Job Search journals. See How Lessons Work.

Open the Graph

Choose View › Graph, press ⌃⌘G, or click Graph in the sidebar. Each person, group, and project in your notes is a dot. The more notes a dot is tagged in, the bigger it is. The more notes two dots share, the closer they sit.

Center a dot

Click a dot to center it. Its page opens in the context pane, and lines join it to the people, groups, and projects it shares the most notes with. Choose how many from the Top dropdown. Press ⎋, or click the empty space, to let go.

Move around

Pinch to zoom, or press + and −. Drag the empty space to move the whole Graph, or drag a dot to move it.

Choose what to see

Use the dropdowns at the top right. Show picks the kinds, such as only people. Tasks shows only those with open tasks. Active shows only those mentioned lately, such as in the last 7 days.
