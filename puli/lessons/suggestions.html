Puli · How Lessons Work

Suggestions.

Puli spots the tags and tasks you didn’t write, so you can accept or decline them.

A lesson

Suggestions is a lesson. Without it, names you write stay as written. You can still tag any word yourself; see Tag a word you already wrote. See How Lessons Work.

Suggested tags

Write “Lunch with Diane about the Grant Report”, and Puli rings “Diane” and “Grant Report”, each in its kind’s color. They also show in the tag rail, below the editor, under Suggestions.

Puli suggests the people, groups, and projects your journal knows, and new people it finds in what you write.

Suggested tasks

A line that reads like something to do, such as “Send Diane the budget”, is suggested as a task. Choose Make a Task to turn it into one.

Accept or decline

Right-click a suggestion, or click it in the tag rail. Choose Tag as and the name, or Make a Task, to accept it. Choose Not a Tag, or Not a Task, to decline it. Puli won’t suggest it in that note again.

To take them all at once, click Accept All ⌘↩ or Ignore All ⇧⌘↩ in the tag rail.
