Puli · How Lessons Work

Today.

See your day beside your notes: your meetings, what’s due, and what you’ve written. Take notes at a meeting in a click.

A lesson

Today is a lesson. It’s free in Job Search journals. See How Lessons Work.

See your day

Choose View › Today, or press ⇧⌘D. Today shows in the context pane, beside your note:

  • Schedule: your meetings, from your Mac’s calendars.
  • Due: the tasks due today, and past due.
  • Today’s note: where tasks you add from outside Puli go.
  • Other notes: the other notes you wrote today.

Click ‹ and › beside the heading to see the day before or after. Click Today to come back. A journal opens on Today.

Show your calendar

The first time, click Show your calendar, and let Puli see your calendars. To choose which ones a journal shows, open Settings › Calendar. Your work journal might show only your work calendar.

A meeting’s page

Click a meeting to see its page: who’s coming, the agenda, and notes from previous meetings in that series. And, on a Mac with Apple Intelligence, there’s a section called Lately to bring you up to date before the meeting.

  • Take Notes starts a note for the meeting, tagging who’s coming. Click it again to get back to that note.
  • Join Meeting opens the associated video conference.

Your day, wherever you are

Click the dog in the menu bar to see today’s meetings. Puli’s widget shows What’s next: the meeting on now, and the rest of today’s.
