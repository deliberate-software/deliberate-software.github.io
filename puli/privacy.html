Puli · Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly.

We don’t collect any data from Puli.

What data do we collect?

None from Puli. No accounts, no analytics, no ads. If you email us, we have your address, and we use it only to write back.

Where are your notes?

On your Mac, encrypted. If you back them up to iCloud, they’re encrypted end to end, with keys only your Macs have. We never see them.

Who do we share data with?

No one. Puli never talks to us. It talks to Apple only when you ask it to:

  • To back up to iCloud.
  • To buy lessons. Apple handles the payment, and tells Puli which lessons you have, never who you are.
  • To send crash reports, if you share analytics with developers in your Mac’s settings. They don’t include your name.

Puli uses Apple Intelligence directly on your Mac. Your notes never leave.

How long do we keep it?

We keep your emails until you ask us to delete them.

What are your rights?

Ask what we have, and we’ll tell you. Ask us to delete it, and we will.

Changes

This policy may change. If it does, we’ll update this page and the date below.
